Actions and Stuff for Minecraft Bedrock

Make every step, swing and swim look the way it should. Actions and Stuff adds smoother player animations, livelier mobs and cleaner particles to Minecraft Bedrock Edition, without changing how the game plays.

Download AddonHow to install

For Minecraft Bedrock (MCPE, Windows, iOS, Android, Xbox). Not for Java Edition.

What is Actions and Stuff?

Actions and Stuff is a visual enhancement pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Instead of adding new game rules, it upgrades the way the game moves and looks: walking, mining, fighting and swimming get natural animations, mobs behave less like repeating loops, and tools and items gain detailed 3D models.

Because it keeps the original blocky style, it fits survival, creative and multiplayer worlds. This page explains what the pack does, which devices it supports, how to install it and what to check when something looks wrong.

Features that change how Minecraft feels

Smooth player animations

Walking, climbing, turning and swimming flow instead of snapping between poses.

Sharper combat

Sword swings, axe hits, blocking and bow draws look weightier and read more clearly.

Livelier mobs

Animals and monsters get improved walk, idle and attack motion, so fields and caves feel active.

Animated items and tools

Eating, mining and raising a shield play out as complete actions.

Detailed 3D models

Tools and objects gain depth while staying true to Minecraft's style.

Better particles

Breaking blocks, landing hits and wading through water produce cleaner effects.

Texture options

Choose lighter textures on older phones and higher detail on strong devices.

Gameplay untouched

Worlds, inventories and mechanics stay exactly as they were.

Activate the pack

  1. Open Minecraft Bedrock and tap the pencil icon beside your world, or create a new world.
  2. Open Resource Packs, then My Packs, and activate Actions and Stuff.
  3. If the download includes a Behavior Pack, activate it too.
  4. If asked, turn on the experimental features listed in the world settings.
  5. Press Play and walk, jump or swim to see the new animations.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • More natural player and mob movement
  • Keeps Minecraft's original look
  • Simple import on Bedrock devices
  • Texture options for weaker hardware

Cons

  • High-detail textures can slow low-end devices
  • May clash with other animation packs
  • New Minecraft updates can break features until the pack updates

Common problems and fixes

Purple, black or missing textures

Remove the pack, import it again and confirm the download finished. Turn off other texture packs to find conflicts.

Animations freeze or look wrong

Disable other animation or visual packs, keep only Actions and Stuff active, then restart the world.

Low FPS or lag

Choose a lighter texture option, lower render distance and close background apps.

Nothing changed after installing

Check that the Resource Pack, and the Behavior Pack if included, are active in this world. Fully close and reopen Minecraft, and confirm your game version matches the release.

Frequently asked questions

What is Actions and Stuff?

A visual enhancement pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that adds smoother player animations, livelier mob movement, detailed item models and better particles.

Is it free?

Community downloads are free. If the pack is sold on the Minecraft Marketplace, its creator sets the price.

Does it work on Java Edition?

No. It is built for Bedrock Edition only.

Does it change gameplay?

No. It changes how the game looks and moves, not its rules.

Can I use it with Vibrant Visuals?

Usually yes. Enable both packs and adjust their order. If textures break, change the order or disable one to find the conflict.
